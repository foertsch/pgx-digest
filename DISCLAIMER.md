# Disclaimer

pgx-digest is a research and educational tool. It is **not a medical device**
and **not a diagnostic**. Output should not be used for clinical
decision-making without consultation with a qualified healthcare
professional.

PharmCAT, the upstream diplotype caller, is research-grade. CPIC
guidelines are clinical-grade, but their application to a specific
genotype requires expert interpretation that this tool does not
provide.

Do not run this software on genomic data you cannot legally analyze.
Genomic data is among the most sensitive personal data and is
subject to GDPR (EU), HIPAA (US, when held by a covered entity),
and other jurisdictional protections.

The author of this software accepts no liability for any use of its
output.
